Harvey is a domain-specific AI platform built for elite law firms and in-house legal departments. Its product suite includes Assistant (question answering, document analysis, and drafting), Vault (secure document storage and bulk analysis), Knowledge (legal, regulatory, and tax research), Workflow Agents (pre-built and customizable legal agents), Harvey Mobile, and an Ecosystem of integrations with existing legal tools. Harvey is sold via enterprise contracts to large law firms and corporate legal teams, and does not currently publish a self-serve public developer API; integrations are arranged through direct sales and partnership engagements.

**Areas:** Artificial Intelligence · Legal Tech · Law Firms · Legal Research · Contract Analysis · Due Diligence · Litigation · Workflow Agents · Enterprise · Vault

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Harvey: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
